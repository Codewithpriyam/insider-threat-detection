01 // MOTIVATION & PROBLEM STATEMENT

THE INSIDER THREAT PARADOX

WHY TRADITIONAL DEFENSES FAIL

  • Firewalls and Network IDS monitor the perimeter against outside attackers.
  • Insiders are already inside: employees, contractors, or compromised legitimate accounts.
  • Network firewalls cannot distinguish between routine business queries and data theft from an authenticated session.

THE PROPOSED UBA SOLUTION

  • User Behavior Analytics (UBA) focuses directly on internal audit logs.
  • Continuous sliding-window evaluation of time, volume, and role changes.
  • Immediate tactical containment before proprietary IP or sensitive records leave the organization.
02 // SCOPE & DETECTION HEURISTICS

THREE CORE ATTACK VECTORS COVERED

VECTOR 01

UNUSUAL ACCESS PATTERNS

  • Off-Hours Access: Logins between 11:00 PM and 5:00 AM.
  • Brute Force / Stuffing: >3 failed logins within 10 minutes.
  • Severity: HIGH / MEDIUM
VECTOR 02

MASS DATA EXFILTRATION

  • Download Spikes: >10 file downloads in rolling 10-min window.
  • Volume Exfiltration: Total transferred bytes exceeding 150 MB.
  • Severity: HIGH
VECTOR 03

PRIVILEGE ESCALATION

  • Unauthorized Roles: Standard user self-assigns ROLE_ADMIN.
  • IAM Tampering: Alteration of access permissions without supervisor sign-off.
  • Severity: CRITICAL

04 // FRONTEND DESIGN SPECIFICATION

VOICEBOX EDITORIAL DESIGN SYSTEM

  • Strict 0px Border-Radius: Angular, square edges across all buttons, cards, dialogs, and tables — no rounded corners.
  • High-Contrast Monochrome: Authoritative #0A0A0A black on #FAFAFA white reminiscent of high-end editorial magazines.
  • Tactical Red Accent (#EF4444): Used strictly like a scalpel for critical incidents, high alerts, and active focus cues.
  • Three Specialized Font Roles: Archivo Black for headlines, Work Sans for controls, and Space Mono for logs.
  • Hero Jump Navigation: Direct top-level links for seamless scrolling between executive metrics and raw audit logs.
